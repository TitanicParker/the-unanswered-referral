# Evidence cross-reference — The Unanswered Referral

*Prepared 18 November 2026 · Local document crosswalk for counsel*

## How to use this guide

The [case overview](THE_UNANSWERED_REFERRAL.md) refers to source notes **[1]–[15]**. The principal 40 numbered documents are copied unchanged into this repository in [the documentary collection](Margaret_Tansey.html). The table below identifies the **local counterpart** to each source note, without claiming that the local compilation reproduces every external item named in the original reference.

Follow the links to examine the cited record. A linked **compiled transcription** is not necessarily the native hospital file. The three photographed handwritten pages and the signed statement have separate files; the statement is retrospective testimony, not a 2017 contemporaneous record.

This is a **navigation and provenance crosswalk**, not an assertion that an inference has been proved by a citation.

## What is and is not closed

**Closed for present navigation:** the numbered original-source compilation, the separate manuscript photographs, the signed statement, and a usable local correspondence between the orientation's 15 source-note groups and the compiled items.

**Not closed as evidential proof:** independently authenticated clinical-system originals, complete 2023 referral and email trail, missing June–December 2025 protest/University correspondence exhibits, full native PAR/SIMT papers, version histories and witnesses. These are explicit requests in the [audit](RECIPIENT_AND_KNOWLEDGE_AUDIT.md), not reasons to delay giving counsel the existing material for review.

**Citation practice:** Where a source category is only partially represented, do not use its compiled link to imply that the unavailable material is already exhibited. Counsel should be able to see the distinction without consulting another repository.
