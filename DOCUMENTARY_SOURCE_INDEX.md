## Source fidelity and limitations

The five copied files listed above have identical GitHub blob hashes to the files in the previous compilation. This verifies exact byte identity of those copies; it does **not** establish native hospital provenance or authenticate editorial transcriptions. The HTML edition is a documentary *compilation*, and its coloured annotations or commentary must not be treated as original medical-record wording. Document numbers and original anchors have not been changed.

The copied files are intentionally stored in the repository root, alongside the counsel materials. Linking them locally avoids depending on the older site's separate orientation or its homepage.
